Traditional Artistry

How We Handmake Your Hat

Every hat is crafted using century-old millinery techniques combined with modern precision sizing.

01

Blocking & Steam

Raw felt or straw is soaked, steamed over boiling kettles, and pulled tightly over hand-carved wooden hat blocks.

02

Shaping & Creasing

Our master hatters hand-crease the crown and expertly curl the brim to achieve your chosen silhouette.

03

Trimming & Banding

Grosgrain ribbons, leather cords, and custom feathers are meticulously hand-stitched onto the hat base.

04

Bespoke Finishing

Italian leather sweatbands are sewn in, hot-foil embossed with your personal initials, and boxed in our signature hat box.

Uncompromising Quality

The Finest Materials on Earth

We source cruelty-free beaver fur, ethical Australian wool, and artisan-woven Ecuadorian palm straw.

Beaver & Rabbit Fur Felt

Incomparably soft, exceptionally water-repellent, and holds its crease through rain or shine for decades.

  • Weatherproof finish
  • Velvet-smooth handfeel
  • Lifetime durability

Hand-Woven Palm Straw

Harvested in Ecuador and tightly hand-braided by master weavers, providing cool shade and lightweight comfort.

  • UPF 50+ UV Sun Protection
  • Flexible & crush-resistant
  • Breathable airflow

Italian Vegetable-Tanned Leather

Used for our internal sweatbands and exterior accent cords. Ages gracefully with a rich patina over time.

  • Supple moisture-wicking fit
  • Custom gold foil engraving
  • Chemical-free tanning
